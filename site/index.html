AI-Powered Continuous Behavioral Biometrics

Prototype of a continuous authentication system that recognises a user by how they move the mouse and type. Browser telemetry becomes 32 statistical features, a Siamese neural network encodes them, and a LightGBM model scores anomalies that can trigger 2FA or lockout.
